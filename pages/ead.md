### **Regenerative Staking**
Stake your EAD to restore harmony.  

The **World Balance Meter** tracks the health of the ecosystem.  
When balance is sustained, rewards grow.  
When imbalance rises, the network self-corrects — burning, redistributing, or regenerating tokens.  

**Every transaction regenerates the world.**

### **Council of Resonance – DAO Governance**
Holders of EAD form the **Council of Resonance**, guiding the evolution of the Eadwyn universe.

The Council votes on:
- Which species, materials, and technologies emerge  
- Which districts expand or transform  
- Which lore chapters unlock next  

This DAO turns world-building into governance — and governance into art.
