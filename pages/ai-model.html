What it is

Open source

Weights, code and training recipes are public. Fork it, audit it, improve it.

Federated

Trained across Pods and homes. Data stays where it grows; only the learning travels.

Grown by everyone

Add knowledge, corrections or compute. Each contribution becomes part of its memory.

Philosophy
"AI supports daily life — managing resources and ensuring transparency — without overshadowing human agency."

An amplifier, not a replacement, of nature's intelligence. Because the model belongs to no one, it can be trusted by everyone: its reasoning is inspectable, its growth is collective, and its failures are lessons the whole federation learns from.
