Five layers
01

Local node layer

Pods, homes, or devices train locally and keep their data local.

02

Coordination layer

Tracks nodes, rounds, versions, sync state, and network metadata.

03

Aggregation layer

Receives updates and creates candidate model merges.

04

Governance layer

Reviews merges, votes, approves, rejects, and publishes.

05

Published model layer

Serves the approved model back to edge inference and the public-facing federation surfaces.
